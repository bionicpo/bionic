- type: points
  eyebrow: KNOW YOUR MOBILITY LEVEL
  title: What Is a K-Level?
  body: A K-level is a rating from K0 to K4 that describes how active you are with a prosthetic leg. Your clinician uses it to choose suitable components, and many insurance plans use it when deciding coverage. Not sure where you fit? We will work it out together during your free evaluation.
  points:
  - "**K0, Non-ambulatory** - Cannot walk or transfer safely, even with help. A prosthesis would not improve mobility."
  - "**K1, Household walker** - Walks or transfers on level ground at a steady pace, mostly at home."
  - "**K2, Limited community walker** - Manages curbs, stairs, and uneven ground at a steady pace."
  - "**K3, Community walker** - Walks at varied speeds and handles most obstacles, work, and exercise."
  - "**K4, High activity** - Goes beyond everyday walking with high impact or stress, as with children, active adults, and athletes."
