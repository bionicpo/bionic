  eyebrow: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
  title: Orthotic Care Questions, Answered
  items:
  - question: Is an orthotic a medical device?
    answer: Yes, orthotics are considered medical devices. They are designed to support, align, or improve the function of specific parts of the body, such as the feet, ankles, or spine, and are typically prescribed by healthcare professionals.
  - question: How long do orthotics last?
    answer: With proper care and regular adjustments, custom orthotics commonly last 1 to 3 years; prefabricated devices vary. Durability depends on factors such as activity level, wear and tear, and the materials used.
  - question: How do you know if you need orthotics?
    answer: If you're experiencing discomfort, foot pain, ankle instability, or difficulty with daily activities like walking or running, orthotics may be helpful. A consultation with a Certified Orthotist can help determine whether a custom or prefabricated device is the right solution for your needs.
  - question: How long does it take to get fitted for an orthotic?
    answer: Prefabricated orthotics can often be fitted in a single visit. Custom orthotics usually take a few weeks, depending on fitting requirements and adjustments.
