  title: Prosthetic Care Questions, Answered
  items:
  - question: How long does it take to get a prosthetic?
    answer: Most people receive their first prosthetic within several weeks to a few months, depending on healing, insurance approval, and fitting needs.
  - question: Is walking with a prosthetic harder than natural walking?
    answer: Walking may initially require more effort, but modern prosthetics are designed to reduce that effort, and many people find walking becomes more efficient over time.
  - question: Do prosthetics require ongoing adjustments?
    answer: Yes. Adjustments are a normal part of prosthetic care, especially during the first year and as activity levels change.
  - question: Will insurance cover a prosthetic?
    answer: Many insurance plans, including Medicare and private insurers, provide prosthetic coverage. Our team helps navigate the process.
  - question: Can I live an active life with a prosthetic?
    answer: Many people return to work, travel, and recreation with the right prosthetic and ongoing support.
