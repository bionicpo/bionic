About Bionic

Why Bionic?

Prosthetics

Artificial limbs that replace a missing arm, leg, hand, or foot.

Orthotics

Custom or prefabricated braces that support, align, and protect your body.

Your doctor prescribes them, and a certified prosthetist or orthotist designs, fits, and adjusts them.

At Bionic, that work is done by certified, state-licensed clinicians who have cared for patients since 2007. Our team includes experienced practitioners and all Bionic facilities are ABC accredited.

We provide services to all age groups living with limb loss or limb difference, and anyone who needs a brace after injury, surgery, stroke, or a spinal condition.
