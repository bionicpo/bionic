      About Bionic

      Why Bionic?

      Prosthetics

      Artificial limbs that replace a missing arm, leg, hand, or foot.

      Orthotics

      Custom or prefabricated braces that support, align, and protect your body.

      Your doctor prescribes them, and a certified prosthetist or orthotist designs, fits, and adjusts them.

      At Bionic, that work is done by certified, state-licensed clinicians who have cared for patients since 2007. Our team includes experienced practitioners and all Bionic facilities are ABC accredited.

      We provide services to all age groups living with limb loss or limb difference, and anyone who needs a brace after injury, surgery, stroke, or a spinal condition.

      How getting a device works

      1. Evaluation

        A certified clinician will examine your limb, strength, mobility, goals, and K-level.

      2. Coverage and Design

        We obtain your prescription, help with insurance approval, and design your device.

      4. Training and follow-up

        We will continue to support you as your goals shift.

      What is the difference between a prosthetist and an orthotist?

      A prosthetist designs and fits prostheses, which are artificial limbs that replace a missing arm, leg, hand, or foot. An orthotist designs and fits orthoses, which are braces that support, align, and protect the body.

      Do I need a prescription to see a prosthetist or orthotist?

      Not to book a consultation. You will need a prescription from your doctor to receive a device, and we can help you get one.

      Are consultations free?

      Yes. All consultations at Bionic are free.

      Can a prosthetist or orthotist visit me at home?

      Yes. If you can't get to a clinic, a Bionic specialist can see you at home, in a nursing facility, or in an assisted living facility at no charge.
